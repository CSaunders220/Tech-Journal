### **Glossary**

_Organizational Unit_ - A group in active directory that can hold and manage users, computers, and groups. These are used to limit the scope of a group policy

_Group Policy_ - A feature of Windows that allows rules and regulations to be defined in the active directory and will apply to the specified users and computers as defined in the policy itself.
